I Don't Have Everything Figured Out.

Learning to be okay with an unfinished map.

I used to think I needed to know exactly where I was going.

What I would become.
Where I would live.
What kind of work I would do.
What the next few years of my life would look like.

It seemed comforting to have a plan.

But somehow, my life has rarely followed one.

There were things I once wanted so badly that I was sure they would become part of my life. Some of them did. Many of them didn't.

And there were also things I never planned for at all.

Becoming a teacher was one of them.

I think that's one of the interesting things about growing up. Sometimes, we spend so much time trying to figure out our future that we forget life is allowed to surprise us.

I have changed my mind more times than I can count.

And perhaps that's not a bad thing.

Maybe changing doesn't always mean that we are lost. Sometimes, it simply means we are learning more about ourselves.

I am still learning.

I am learning that I don't have to have every answer before taking the next step.

That I can be uncertain and still move forward.

That I can have a dream without knowing exactly how I will get there.

And that a different path doesn't necessarily mean a wrong one.

There are still many things I want to do. Places I want to see. Things I want to learn. People I want to meet. And perhaps there are versions of myself that I haven't met yet.

I don't know exactly where this journey will take me.

But I think I'm slowly becoming more comfortable with that.

For now, I just want to keep learning, keep becoming, and keep showing up for the life that is in front of me.

I may not have everything figured out.

Perhaps that's the point.

Maybe I don't need a complete map. Maybe I just need enough light to see the next step.

And for now, that's enough.

I hope Allah guides every step of the way.
